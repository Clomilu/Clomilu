Clomilu uses cookies and similar technologies to operate its website and service, keep the service secure, and remember certain user preferences.

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how Clomilu uses them, and how you can manage your preferences.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They allow a website to recognise your device and, depending on their purpose, remember information about your session or preferences.

Clomilu may also use similar technologies, such as local storage, where necessary for the operation of the service.

2. How Clomilu uses cookies

We use only cookies and similar technologies that are necessary for the operation, security and functionality of Clomilu, or that you have chosen to enable.

These may include:

Strictly necessary technologies

These technologies are required for the website and service to function properly. They may be used to:

  • maintain your session and authentication;
  • keep the service secure;
  • remember essential technical settings;
  • enable core features of the Clomilu service.

These technologies cannot be disabled through the cookie settings where they are technically necessary for the service to operate.

Preference technologies

Where applicable, Clomilu may use cookies or local storage to remember choices you make, such as your selected language or other interface preferences.

If you change a preference manually, we may store that choice so that it can be applied during your future visits.

Analytics and other optional technologies

Clomilu does not require advertising cookies or third-party tracking technologies to provide its core service.

If optional analytics or other non-essential technologies are introduced in the future, they will be enabled only in accordance with applicable legal requirements and, where required, with your prior consent. This Cookie Policy will be updated accordingly.

3. Third-party services

Some technical services used to operate Clomilu may place or access cookies or similar technologies on your device.

Where this occurs, the relevant provider may process certain technical information in accordance with its own privacy policy and applicable contractual arrangements.

Clomilu does not use cookies for the purpose of selling personal information or displaying behavioural advertising.

4. Managing your cookie preferences

When non-essential cookies are used, you can manage your preferences through the Cookie Settings interface available on the website.

You can also configure your browser to block or delete cookies. However, disabling strictly necessary cookies may prevent certain parts of Clomilu from functioning correctly.

Your cookie preferences are separate from your Clomilu account and service preferences.

5. Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy when our website, service or use of cookies changes, or when required by applicable law.

The latest version will always be available on this page, with the date of the latest update shown at the top.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about our use of cookies or your privacy, you can contact us using the contact details provided in the Legal Notice or Privacy Policy.
